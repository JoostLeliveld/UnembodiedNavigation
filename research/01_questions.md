# Immutable research questions

These questions form the thesis spine. Experiments may change; the questions do not.

## SQ1 — Representation

How should observation quality be represented?

The representation separates availability from conditional accuracy and retains systematic
error, epistemic support, and runtime health rather than collapsing everything into one
trust score.

## SQ2 — Estimation

How should observation quality be estimated?

The comparison is between geometric, learned, and hybrid estimators under an explicit
operational-information contract.

## SQ3 — Planning

How should observation quality influence planning?

Planning must propagate the probability of receiving a usable update and the covariance of
that update. Offline route discrimination is required before simulator campaign time.

## SQ4 — Deployment trade-offs

What deployment trade-offs exist?

The thesis reports commissioning effort, cold-start ability, transfer, adaptation, runtime,
failure modes, and fallback—not just prediction accuracy.
