# Exact prerequisites for combined successor acceptance

Completed component boundaries remain complete when their owned hashes still match. A
different owner integration gap does not reopen them. The deterministic acceptance gate
opens only after these source-bound handoffs exist:

1. Owner 01 supplies the final belief/state/correction schema implementation, exact hashes
   and passing serialized-transaction, outage-motion, reset and A/B regression commands.
   Owner 02 then runs its independent desired-invariant packet on those exact hashes. The
   three expanded 03/01 transaction failures must be closed or remain failing evidence.
2. Owner 07 proves one manager transaction consumes the 01 operational belief and preserves
   04 cycle/frame/invocation membership through 05 validation, admission, fusion and the
   correction envelope. Owner 06 independently closes rejection/recovery, turn and
   missing-motion cases on that source.
3. Owner 08 hands off the complete finite solver-result contract. Owners 03 and 09 verify
   request/goal/config/epoch identity, supported newer-belief revalidation, command stop
   precedence and mission terminal separation against the final 01 API.
4. Owner 10 proves schema-8 raw delivery/publication/terminal accounting, detector-journal
   recovery after producer quiescence, independent close failures, queue drain and atomic
   final summary. Owner 11 proves `aligned.py` validates that raw ledger before truth
   filtering, binds exact posterior/reference support, preserves reasoned refusals and
   rejects wrong/incomplete selections.
5. Owner 12 proves capture resume/export and exact model/calibration/profile identity.
   Owner 13 supplies a hash-complete campaign/launch handoff and closes its declared joins
   to 07/10/12/14. Dry command construction is component evidence, not startup evidence.
6. Owner 14 supplies a hash-complete component handoff for the native guard, launch wiring,
   reset refusal, collision/contact closure and its private probes. Its current guard result
   is explicitly `forwarded`, not actuator application. Existing timeout/contact probes do
   not replace the final post-component scenario because they do not exercise S1-S10 as one
   reconstructible evidence interval or establish sustained physical rest.
7. After source freeze, the coordinator completes active README, HOW_IT_WORKS, runtime audit,
   registry, protocol and active caption reconciliation. Author-facing thesis/paper prose
   review remains a separately disclosed item under workspace writing rules and does not
   permit rewriting historical scientific evidence.

The coordinator then issues `READY.json` with the exact source identity, resolved config
identity, schema version, versioned batch/invocation contract and the SHA-256 of the final
`owner_handoffs.json`. The deterministic runner executes desired owner tests plus the
independent ROS-free cross-chain assertions. Any source hash drift closes the gate.

Only after that suite passes may owner 14 provide one isolated simulator command. The run
must cover S1-S10 without becoming a campaign and must preserve raw logs through shutdown.
It must produce the adapter fixture in `recorded_chain_schema.md`; the final stage runs
`test_recorded_cross_chain_evidence.py` and records `aligned.py`'s explicit acceptance or
refusal. A successful drive alone does not establish a camera-model or planning gain.
