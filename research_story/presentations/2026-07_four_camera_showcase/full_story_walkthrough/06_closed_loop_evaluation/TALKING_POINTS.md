# 06 — Camera overlay and switching

This is the first multi-camera slide. It will show the four camera footprints
over the warehouse, a deliberately enlarged overlap corridor, and a route
through that corridor. A parallel source timeline will show the intended
manager behaviour: retain the current source while its trust is adequate,
switch only after the next source is consistently better, and defer when no
source is trustworthy.

This is an intended-operation visual until the repeated overlap campaign is
complete; it must not be described as a completed fusion result.
