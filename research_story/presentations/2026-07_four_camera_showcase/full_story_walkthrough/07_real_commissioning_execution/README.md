# 07 — Real commissioning execution

This is the evidence chapter that replaces the earlier “data pending” promise.
It is based on two actual runs in `warehouse_full_4cam`:

1. a south-to-north handover traverse; and
2. a dedicated Camera A/C horizontal-overlap pass.

The figures are generated by:

```bash
python3 experiments/multicamera_commissioning_bigwarehouse/tools/render_actual_commissioning_showcase.py
```

Figures 01–05 consume only operational detector records, noisy odometry, the
day-zero prior, and the canonical expected-kernel GP artifacts. They never use
Gazebo ground truth for fitting or policy input. Figure 06 is explicitly
different: it is an independent evaluation-only readiness audit and may use
ground truth solely to score the pilot's projection and covariance behaviour.

Open [TALKING_POINTS.md](TALKING_POINTS.md) when presenting the figures.
