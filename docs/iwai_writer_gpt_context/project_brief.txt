Project brief for IWAI paper drafting
=====================================

Working topic:
Visibility-aware Expected Free Energy navigation under fixed external-camera observations.

Current thesis-report title:
Active Inference Navigation Under External Camera Observations

One-sentence problem:
A mobile robot may choose a collision-free route through a region where an external camera detector gives weak or intermittent position updates, so planning should account for future observation reliability rather than only geometric progress.

Core method:
Use a receding-horizon EFE planner in belief space.
The learned external-camera observability model changes the predictive observation covariance \(R_{\mathrm{eff}}\).
It is not a direct visibility reward, route penalty, hard visibility constraint, or shadow heuristic.

Current runtime signal path:
1. Gazebo external RGB camera observes a TurtleBot-like robot in `warehouse_occ_light.world.sdf`.
2. YOLO segmentation returns candidate masks, selected image point, and raw detector score.
3. The selected bottom image point is projected to planar position through homography.
4. Heading is supplied by odometry; YOLO does not currently provide reliable yaw.
5. Offline detector-score captures are averaged over headings at each \((x,y)\).
6. A 2D heading-marginal RBF GP is fit in logit space.
7. The planner uses \( \rho_{\mathrm{plan}}=\sigma(\mu_h-\beta\sigma_h) \).
8. \( \rho_{\mathrm{plan}} \) is queried over predicted belief sigma points.
9. \(R_{\mathrm{eff}}\) is obtained by precision-blending \(R_{\mathrm{visible}}\) and \(R_{\mathrm{miss}}\).
10. \(R_{\mathrm{eff}}\) affects predicted observation covariance, EFE risk, EFE ambiguity, and predicted covariance updates.

Known caveats:
- YOLO raw score is uncalibrated.
- The GP is 2D and heading-marginal.
- The estimator uses camera-derived \(x,y\) and odometry-derived yaw.
- Gazebo dynamics are unrealistically accurate unless an explicit actuation-noise node is added.
- Planner \(Q\) inflates belief only; it does not physically disturb the robot.
- Solve time and pixel-correction age can affect runtime behavior.
- Current logger separates goal-region entry from completion reason. Stable or idle
  behavior inside the goal region terminates as goal-reaching; `stuck` is reserved
  for lack of displacement and lack of goal progress outside the goal region.
- Older logs that ended as near-goal `stuck` remain legacy case-study evidence only.
- Older `main_shadow_tradeoff` runs use legacy geometry and should not be treated as final benchmark evidence.
