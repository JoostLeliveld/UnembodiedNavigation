Claims and caveats ledger
=========================

Allowed claims now:
- The implementation can produce a valid collision-free route through the lower side of the occluding shelf region using a learned YOLO-score GP and no direct visibility reward.
- The learned observability field enters the EFE planner through the effective observation covariance.
- The GP provides both a smooth mean detectability estimate and a conservative uncertainty-discounted planner map.
- Current completed runs reach the goal region with no wall or obstacle penetration.
- The current best configuration is promising enough to motivate a predefined experiment campaign.

Claims that require the full experiment campaign:
- Learned observability is statistically better than constant observability.
- EFE outperforms MPC or any classical baseline.
- The method robustly avoids occlusion.
- The system is real-world ready.
- The YOLO score is a calibrated visibility probability.

Do not claim:
- "The planner uses a visibility reward" for the main case-study run.
  Correct: the paper planner has no direct visibility reward; learned observability affects `R_eff`, risk, and ambiguity.
- "The GP models heading."
  Correct: the current GP is 2D and heading-marginal.
- "The latest interrupted run is a successful completed run."
  Correct: `experiment_20260423_112814` is valid so far but interrupted.
- "Stuck means failure to reach the goal."
  Correct: in older case-study runs, minimum goal distance is very small and `stuck` appears near/after goal-region arrival. Current logging records goal-region entry separately and terminates stable/idle goal behavior as goal-reaching.

Main caveats:
- YOLO raw confidence is uncalibrated and detector-specific.
- The current GP averages heading-dependent visibility effects.
- The current state estimator uses visual XY and odometry heading; it is not a full visual pose estimator.
- Pixel correction age can become large, suggesting runtime scheduling/callback timing issues.
- Solve time is still high for a control loop.
- Near-goal risk can spike due to covariance/log-det mismatch when the planner predicts almost zero terminal goal distance.
- Safety barriers, covariance clipping, conservative beta, and optimizer limits are engineering guards and should be disclosed.
- Gazebo dynamics are unrealistically accurate unless actuation noise is added explicitly; planner process noise \(Q\) only inflates belief.
- Runtime route-family multistart seeding has been removed from paper code. If route families are discussed, call them offline rollout probes.

Current case-study wording:
"A representative completed run reaches within 0.039 m of the goal without collision or penetration, but is logged as `stuck` after entering the goal region. We therefore treat this as a valid near-success case study and use it to inspect the signal pathway, while leaving success-rate claims to the planned multi-seed comparison."

Current stopping-logic wording:
"Current runs separate goal-region entry from completion reason. Stable or idle behavior inside the goal region terminates as goal-reaching, while `stuck` is reserved for lack of displacement and lack of goal progress outside the goal region. Older near-goal `stuck` logs are legacy case-study evidence."

Current method wording:
"The learned map is not a direct reward. It modulates the predictive observation covariance used in the EFE rollout. Poor predicted observability makes future observations less informative, which affects risk and ambiguity."

Current experiment-planning wording:
"The main comparison should be between a constant-observability EFE baseline and the learned YOLO-score-GP EFE planner under matched dynamics, estimator, detector, safety geometry, optimizer limits, and stopping logic."
